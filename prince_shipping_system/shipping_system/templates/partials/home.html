About Prince Shipping

Prince Shipping was founded in 1997 with the goal of providing Zimbabwean businesses with efficient and reliable shipping solutions. The company has grown steadily over the years and offers a wide range of services including customs clearance, transport and logistics, consolidating and warehousing, rebates, import licenses and permits application, and tax clearance and company registration.
